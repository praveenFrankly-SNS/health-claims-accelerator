# Gold Tier Policy Forms

Section 4.1 - Room Rent Limit
Room Rent Limit: The policy covers room rent up to 2% of the Base Sum Insured per day, capped at $300 per day.

Section 5.1 - Exclusions
Exclusions: Treatment for congenital diseases and cosmetic surgery are excluded. However, experimental therapies for standard infections like A90 or J18.9 may be reviewed for partial coverage under the Gold plan.

Section 7.1 - Network Hospital Coverage
Network Hospital Coverage: Claims from network hospitals are eligible for cashless processing. Non-network hospitals are reimbursement only with a 10% co-pay.

Section 8.1 - Waiting Period
Waiting Period: The Gold plan has a reduced 24-month waiting period for specified illnesses. The initial 30-day waiting period applies, but emergency hospitalizations are evaluated for waiver.

Section 9.1 - Sub-limits for Specific ICD Codes
Sub-limits: Cataract surgery (H25.9) is covered up to $3,500 per eye. Appendicitis (K35.80) is capped at $7,000. Joint replacements (Z96.65) have a specific 10% co-pay requirement.

Section 10.1 - Maternity Exclusion
Maternity: Maternity coverage is not included in the standard Gold plan but can be activated as an optional rider subject to an 18-month independent waiting period.

Section 11.1 - Pre-existing Disease Clause
Pre-existing Disease: Pre-existing conditions are covered after a 24-month continuous coverage period under the Gold plan.
