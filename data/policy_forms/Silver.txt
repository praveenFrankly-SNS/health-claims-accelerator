# Silver Tier Policy Forms

Section 4.1 - Room Rent Limit
Room Rent Limit: The policy covers room rent up to 1% of the Base Sum Insured per day, capped at $150 per day.

Section 5.1 - Exclusions
Exclusions: Treatment for congenital diseases, cosmetic surgery, and unproven treatments are excluded from the Silver plan. Experimental therapies for J18.9 (Pneumonia) and A90 (Dengue) are not covered.

Section 7.1 - Network Hospital Coverage
Network Hospital Coverage: Claims from network hospitals are eligible for cashless processing. Non-network hospitals are reimbursement only with a 20% co-pay for the Silver tier.

Section 8.1 - Waiting Period
Waiting Period: The Silver plan requires a 48-month waiting period for all specified illnesses and a 30-day initial waiting period for any hospital admission except in the case of accidental injury.

Section 9.1 - Sub-limits for Specific ICD Codes
Sub-limits: Treatments related to ICD code H25.9 (Senile cataract) are capped at $1,500 per eye. Appendicitis (K35.80) is capped at $3,000 including surgeon fees and room rent.

Section 10.1 - Maternity Exclusion
Maternity: All maternity, childbirth, and related complications are explicitly excluded from the Silver tier. No coverage will be provided under any circumstances.

Section 11.1 - Pre-existing Disease Clause
Pre-existing Disease: Any medical condition diagnosed within 48 months prior to the policy inception date is classified as a pre-existing disease and will not be covered under the Silver plan until the waiting period elapses.
